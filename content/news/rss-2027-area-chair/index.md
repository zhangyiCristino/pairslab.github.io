PAIRS Lab Director **Professor Fangqiang Ding** will serve as an **Area Chair for Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS) 2027**, which will take place in **Athens, Greece, on July 6–11, 2027**.

## Reviewer recruitment

Prof. Ding is currently recruiting researchers for his reviewer pool in the following areas:

- **Robot Perception, Sensing & Vision**
- **Autonomous Navigation & Mobile Robotics**
- **Grasping & Manipulation**
- **Robot Learning & Foundation Models**

We particularly welcome **postdoctoral researchers, junior faculty, and experienced senior PhD students** with strong research backgrounds and reviewing experience.

**Reviewer signup deadline for this pool: November 10, 2026.**

Interested researchers are invited to contact Prof. Ding at [**fangqiangd@hkust-gz.edu.cn**](mailto:fangqiangd@hkust-gz.edu.cn).

We look forward to working with the community to support a rigorous, constructive, and high-quality review process for RSS 2027.

## About RSS and the Area Chair role

RSS is a leading international robotics conference, bringing together research on the scientific foundations, algorithms, and systems of robotics in a focused, single-track program.

Area Chairs carry substantive responsibility for scientific assessment and the quality of peer review. Under the [RSS 2027 review process](https://roboticsconference.org/information/cfp/), reviewers and Area Chairs assess research hypotheses and methodologies. Area Chairs also refine the meta-review, taking the authors' responses into account, into an Extension Charter that sets out the work required for the next submission stage. Serving in this role calls for deep subject expertise, sound scientific judgment, and a commitment to fair, constructive evaluation.
