**PAIRS-Pi**, a student team from PAIRS Lab at HKUST(GZ), received **Second Prize** at the finals of the **2026 Safety and Emergency Rescue Robotics Competition in Guangzhou**. The team was advised by **Prof. Fangqiang Ding**.

The finals and results presentation were held in Guangzhou on October 23–24. In the post-disaster mountainous-terrain supply-delivery event, the team's quadruped robot carried a **25 kg payload** across obstacles including rubble, ditches, and scattered tree trunks, as reported by [Wen Wei Po](https://www.wenweipo.com/s/202610/24/AP6adcbff5e4b0fa89498a32ef.html). The challenge brought robot perception, locomotion, and control into a demanding outdoor rescue scenario.

This result follows the lab's [earlier recognition in the Creative Competition]({{< relref "news/rrc-awards-2026" >}}). Visit the [official competition media page](https://global-rrc.com/media) for event media.
