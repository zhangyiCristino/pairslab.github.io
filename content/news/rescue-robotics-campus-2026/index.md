On November 26, 2026, PAIRS Lab teams **PAIRS-Pi** and **PAIRS-Rescue** shared their experience from the 2026 Safety and Emergency Rescue Robotics Competition at a campus event at HKUST(GZ). China Daily published coverage of the event the following day.

Team representatives **Ruipeng Yang** and **Yinuo Chen** described how the teams, advised by **Professor Fangqiang Ding**, took their work from the lab to the competition field. Prof. Ding also joined a panel with representatives from industry, government, and the university to discuss practical rescue robotics and how AI could support emergency preparedness on campus and in laboratories.
