Jiashen Liu is a Research Assistant at the PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), China. He received his M.Sc. in Cyber Security Engineering with Distinction from the University of Warwick in 2025. His research interests lie in Computer Vision, Physical AI simulation, and 3D reconstruction, with a particular focus on leveraging real-world robotic interactions to improve simulation-ready 3D assets and bridge the gap between simulation and reality.
