Sizhen He received an MPhil in Data Intensive Science from the University of Cambridge. He is currently a Research Assistant at PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). His research interests include embodied AI, vision-language-action models, world models, and memory-augmented learning for long-horizon robotic manipulation.
