Yuzhou Chen is an MSc student in Smart Manufacturing at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) and a member of PAIRS Lab. He earned a BEng in Robotics Engineering from South China University of Technology, where he reconstructed 3D hand and object trajectories from video for his undergraduate thesis. He is exploring how to [turn first-person videos of people handling objects into training data for robot hands](https://human-to-robot-data.scut-lang.chatgpt.site/en). He is particularly interested in whether fingertip feedback can help a robot hand keep hold of an object as it repositions its fingers.
